# Scope and Non-Scope

> Status: Baseline V1 — subject to final architecture audit.

## Purpose
Prevent scope creep.

## In scope

Proprietary trading automation, AI company OS, multi-market research/trading, market intelligence, risk, execution, monitoring and governance.

## Out of scope V1

Public SaaS, customers, billing, multi-tenancy, social/copy trading, public strategy marketplace, unrestricted agent autonomy and legal/compliance claims.
